# MediBook differentiation: Calm Orbit

MediBook now has a product direction that is intentionally different from generic appointment-booking apps. The experience is organized around **Care Compass**, a calm command center that answers one question first: “What is the best next action for my care?”

## Shipped in this wave

| Experience | Why it stands out |
|---|---|
| Care Compass | Replaces a generic module grid with a contextual next-best-action timeline. |
| Visit Readiness | Turns appointment preparation into a short, supportive checklist rather than a blank booking confirmation. |
| Family Care | Makes multi-person care explicit while keeping sharing permissions visible. |
| Smart Waitlist | Treats waiting as a preference-controlled service with quiet notifications and no public queue number. |
| Calm Orbit visual system | Uses a distinctive navy/teal/mint gradient, generous touch targets, and low-anxiety language. |
| Arabic-first parity | New feature labels and guidance ship in Arabic and English together. |

## Product rails for the next release

The architecture is ready for additional differentiators without coupling them to the presentation layer: a patient-owned care timeline, consent receipts for every shared record, explainable appointment recommendations, caregiver delegation with expiration dates, outcome check-ins after visits, multilingual voice notes with explicit transcription consent, medication adherence insights, accessible large-type mode, and clinic-side operational heatmaps.

These features should remain server-authoritative for clinical facts and permission decisions. Recommendation or wellness features must be explainable, optional, and clearly separated from diagnosis. Any reminder or notification must be quiet by default, user-configurable, and auditable.
